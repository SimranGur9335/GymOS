# GymOS Blueprint

## Purpose
This folder is the master product and engineering blueprint for GymOS.

## Product
**GymOS — Run Your Gym. Grow Your Business. Find Your Fitness.**

GymOS is planned as a multi-tenant SaaS + fitness discovery platform for the Indian gym/fitness market.

## Two product sides
- **GymOS Business:** operating and growth platform for gym owners, managers and trainers.
- **GymOS Discover:** public discovery, comparison and enquiry experience for people looking for gyms.

## Core lifecycle
Discover → Search → Compare → Enquire → Join → Manage → Pay → Attend → Retain → Grow

## Core UX principle
**See → Understand → Act → Measure**

## Core business principle
Every important feature should increase revenue, recover revenue, reduce churn, save staff time, or generate leads.

## Core technical principle
Multi-tenant from day one. One GymOS platform, many gyms, completely isolated data.

## Development principle
Design the complete platform now. Build and validate V1 first.

## Source basis
This blueprint is derived from the GymOS Complete Product Blueprint Context document supplied for this project.
