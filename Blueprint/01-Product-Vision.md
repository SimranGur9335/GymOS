# Product Vision

## Vision
GymOS is a complete operating and growth platform for gyms while also helping people discover, compare and connect with gyms.

## Positioning
GymOS should not be positioned as another basic gym management system.

It combines:
1. Gym operations
2. Business growth
3. Retention
4. Communication
5. Fitness discovery

## Business outcomes
GymOS should help gyms:
- Increase revenue through lead conversion, renewals, upselling, PT and offers.
- Recover lost revenue through pending/overdue payments and expiring memberships.
- Reduce churn through inactivity detection, retention alerts and re-engagement.
- Save staff time through automation, reminders and centralized data.
- Give owners visibility into revenue, attendance, memberships, leads and retention.

## Signature experience
The owner should not merely see charts. GymOS should tell the owner what needs attention today and provide an action.

Example:
Pending payment → identify member → send WhatsApp → payment received → ROI visible.

## Product flywheel
Discover → Enquire → Join → Pay → Attend → Engage → Renew → Retain → Grow
