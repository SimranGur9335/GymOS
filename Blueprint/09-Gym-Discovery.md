# Gym Discovery

## Search
Users can search by:
- Gym name
- City
- Area
- Location
- Price
- Rating
- Amenities
- Distance
- Gym type
- Open now
- Personal training
- Parking

## Natural-language discovery
Example:
“Show gyms near Kothrud under ₹2,000 with parking and good reviews.”

## Public listing
A listing may contain:
- Gym name
- Location
- Rating
- Price
- Directions
- Contact
- Compare
- Enquire
- About
- Amenities
- Photos
- Videos

## Comparison
Comparison should transparently show attributes such as:
- Price
- Rating
- Parking
- Shower
- Cardio
- PT
- Photos
- Videos

GymOS should not automatically declare one gym “best”; present transparent comparison.

## Claiming
Unclaimed listing → owner verifies ownership → owner controls listing information → verified listing.
