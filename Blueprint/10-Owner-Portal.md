# Owner Portal

## Dashboard
Primary purpose: tell the owner what needs attention today.

## Core modules
- Members
- Memberships
- Payments
- Attendance
- Leads
- Trainers
- Workouts
- Marketing
- WhatsApp
- Retention
- Analytics
- AI Insights
- Settings

## Member management
Owner/staff can:
- Add
- Edit
- Delete/deactivate
- Search/filter
- View profile
- View membership
- View payment history
- View attendance
- View trainer
- View workout
- View progress
- View measurements
- Add notes
- View emergency contact
- View membership history

## Business action model
Every important alert should lead to a direct action rather than only displaying a metric.
