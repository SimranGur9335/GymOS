# AI and ML

## Philosophy
AI should not be a generic “ChatGPT chatbot for gym”.

AI should tell the owner what needs attention.

## AI business insights
Examples:
- Members inactive for 10+ days
- Revenue at risk
- Leads not contacted in 48 hours
- Peak hours

Every insight should ideally provide a Take Action option.

## Retention
Start with rules, not ML.

Example:
If last visit > 10 days AND normal frequency > 3 visits/week → retention alert.

Later:
AI retention prediction with estimated churn risk and reasons.

## Revenue forecasting
Future:
- Expected revenue
- Expected renewals
- At-risk revenue
- Explanation for forecast

## Workout generation
AI may eventually generate a draft workout, but trainer must review and approve it.

## ML requirements
ML churn prediction requires sufficient historical data. New gyms need a rule-based fallback.

## Technology
- Gemini API
- Python
- scikit-learn
- XGBoost later
