# Market Research

## Validation required before serious development
Phase 0 explicitly requires conversations with 10–20 real gyms.

## Questions to validate
- How is the gym currently tracking members?
- What software is currently used?
- What does the gym currently pay for software?
- What is the biggest operational pain?
- Is WhatsApp automation valuable?
- Is payment management valuable?
- Is retention intelligence valuable?
- What is the willingness to pay?

## Validation principle
The pricing in the source document is an initial hypothesis and must be validated with real gyms before launch.

## Initial pricing hypothesis
- Starter: ₹499/month
- Growth: ₹1,499/month
- Pro: ₹2,999/month
- Multi-Branch: ₹5,999/month

- Setup/data migration
